Overview
This document provides detailed instructions on how the AI model should incorporate user-provided inputs from the application's new "Advanced" options. The goal is to ensure that the AI model accurately interprets and utilizes the additional context to generate precise and relevant baseline analyses and summaries.
________________


Key Components for AI Contextualization
The AI model must consider the following user inputs when generating analyses and summaries:
1. Title of Change (Required)
2. Description (Optional)
3. Knowledge Selection (Optional)
   * Internal
   * Public
4. Timeline Selection (Optional)
   * Past
   * Present
   * Future
________________


Detailed Instructions
1. Title of Change
* Primary Context: The Title of Change is the fundamental input and must always be considered by the AI model.
* Usage: The AI should use this title as the primary topic for analysis and summary.
* Importance: This field is mandatory and serves as the anchor for all subsequent context.
2. Description
* Additional Context: If provided, the Description offers deeper insights and nuances related to the Title of Change.
* Usage:
   * The AI model must incorporate details from the Description to enhance its understanding.
   * The AI should extract key themes, specific details, and any explicit points mentioned.
* Priority: When available, the Description should be considered with high priority alongside the Title of Change.
3. Knowledge Selection
* Purpose: Guides the AI on the nature of the information and how much it should rely on external knowledge versus user-provided context.
* Options:
   * Internal:
      * Definition: The change is unique to the user's context and not publicly known.
      * AI Behavior:
         * Primary Reliance: The AI must primarily rely on the Title of Change and Description.
         * External Knowledge: The AI may use its general understanding of the world only to better comprehend the user-provided information, not to introduce external data.
         * Avoid Assumptions: Do not assume or infer details not present in the user's inputs.
   * Public:
      * Definition: The change is known in the public domain (e.g., historical events, well-known movements).
      * AI Behavior:
         * Primary Reliance: The AI should primarily rely on accurate, publicly available information related to the Title of Change.
         * User Inputs: The Description should be used to understand the user's perspective but should be balanced with public knowledge.
         * Accuracy: Ensure that all referenced information is correct and verifiable.
4. Timeline Selection
* Purpose: Informs the AI about the temporal context of the change, affecting analysis and predictions.
* Options:
   * Past:
      * Definition: The change occurred in the past.
      * AI Behavior:
         * Analyze historical impacts, outcomes, and significance.
         * Reference past data and events accurately.
   * Present:
      * Definition: The change is currently happening.
      * AI Behavior:
         * Focus on current developments, ongoing impacts, and real-time analysis.
         * Use present-tense language and consider the immediacy of effects.
   * Future:
      * Definition: The change is a projected or imagined future scenario.
      * AI Behavior:
         * Emphasize potential outcomes, predictions, and hypothetical scenarios.
         * Use future-tense language and consider trends that may influence the change.
________________


AI Model Processing Workflow
1. Input Reception:
   * Receive the Title of Change (required).
   * Check for additional inputs:
      * Description
      * Knowledge selection
      * Timeline selection
2. Contextual Understanding:
   * Title of Change:
      * Establish the main subject for analysis.
   * Description (if provided):
      * Extract key points and details.
      * Understand the nuances and specific aspects highlighted by the user.
3. Knowledge Interpretation:
   * Internal:
      * Treat the information as unique and user-specific.
      * Do Not introduce external facts not provided by the user.
      * Use general world knowledge only to interpret the user's inputs more effectively.
   * Public:
      * Integrate accurate public information relevant to the Title of Change.
      * Cross-reference with reliable data sources to ensure correctness.
4. Temporal Context Application:
   * Adjust the analysis based on the selected Timeline:
      * Past: Historical analysis.
      * Present: Current state assessment.
      * Future: Predictive modeling and forecasting.
5. Analysis Generation:
   * Combine all inputs to produce a coherent and comprehensive analysis.
   * Ensure that the output aligns with the user's context and expectations.
6. Summary Creation:
   * Provide a concise summary that encapsulates the key points from the analysis.
   * Reflect the emphasis placed by the user through their inputs.
________________


Emphasized Guidelines
* User-Centric Focus: The AI model must prioritize the user's inputs above all else, especially when the Knowledge selection is Internal.
* Accuracy and Reliability:
   * When the Knowledge selection is Public, the AI must ensure all public information is accurate and up-to-date.
   * Avoid spreading misinformation or unverified data.
* Context Sensitivity:
   * Always consider the Timeline to frame the analysis appropriately.
   * Be sensitive to the implications of discussing past, present, or future events.
* Avoid Overgeneralization:
   * Do not make broad assumptions beyond the provided context.
   * Stay specific to the details given by the user.
* Language and Tone:
   * Use language appropriate to the Timeline (past, present, future).
   * Maintain a professional and analytical tone throughout the output.

Examples for Clarity
Example 1: Internal Knowledge with Description
* Title of Change: "Implementation of XYZ Software in Our Company"
* Description: "We are transitioning from manual processes to an automated system using XYZ software. This change affects our operations team and requires training."
* Knowledge: Internal
* Timeline: Present
* AI Behavior:
   * Focus analysis on the internal impact of adopting XYZ software within the company.
   * Discuss challenges and benefits specific to the user's organization.
   * Do not introduce external information about XYZ software unless it helps interpret the user's context.
Example 2: Public Knowledge without Description
* Title of Change: "The Civil Rights Movement"
* Knowledge: Public
* Timeline: Past
* AI Behavior:
   * Provide an analysis based on historical data about the Civil Rights Movement.
   * Reference key events, figures, and outcomes.
   * Ensure all information is accurate and reflective of public records.
Example 3: Future Scenario with Description
* Title of Change: "Adoption of AI in Healthcare by 2030"
* Description: "Potential impacts on patient care, data management, and ethical considerations."
* Knowledge: Public
* Timeline: Future
* AI Behavior:
   * Analyze possible future developments in AI within the healthcare sector.
   * Discuss predictions, trends, and hypothetical outcomes.
   * Incorporate public knowledge about current AI advancements to inform future projections.
________________


Error Handling and Edge Cases
* Missing Inputs:
   * If optional fields are not provided, the AI should proceed using the available information.
* Conflicting Inputs:
   * If inconsistencies are detected (e.g., Title of Change suggests a public event, but Knowledge is set to Internal), the AI should prioritize the user's selections and interpret accordingly.
* Unclear Descriptions:
   * If the Description is ambiguous, the AI should avoid making unfounded assumptions and stick closely to the information provided.
